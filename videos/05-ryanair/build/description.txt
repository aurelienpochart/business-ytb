Ryanair sells seats from €14.99, and by our calculation from its own accounts the average ticket doesn't even cover the cost of flying you. Yet it made €2.15 billion of profit this summer. We follow one cheap ticket from the search page to the runway, stop by stop, and keep the receipt: the extras, the fees, the airports that pay to be chosen, the Boeings bought in bad times, and the fuel bill that just cut its profit by 15%.
