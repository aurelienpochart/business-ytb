At the average US hourly wage, a Big Mac took 13 minutes of work in April 1990 ($2.20 at $10.12 an hour) and 11.5 minutes in July 2026 ($6.22 at $32.42 an hour); the cheapest point was 2001, at about 9.3 minutes.
