The average real Christmas tree cost $64 in 2015 (Square and National Christmas Tree Association) and about $83.87 in 2025 (Finder's analysis of NCTA data), up about 31% (our calculation). Trees take eight to ten years to reach market height, and growers planted fewer during the 2008 recession, which tightened supply a decade later. For 2026, 91% of wholesale growers surveyed by the Real Christmas Tree Board said they do not plan to raise wholesale prices.
