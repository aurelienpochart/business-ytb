Over the 2025 Thanksgiving weekend, a record 202.9 million Americans shopped (NRF and Prosper Insights survey); US online spending from Thanksgiving to Cyber Monday reached $44.2 billion, with Cyber Monday at $14.25 billion and $1.03 billion of Cyber Monday spending on buy now, pay later (Adobe Analytics). Adobe forecasts $47.5 billion for Cyber Week 2026, about $6.6 million a minute over five days (our calculation).
