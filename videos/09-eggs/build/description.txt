A dozen eggs hit a record $6.23 in March 2025. Bird flu was the official explanation, and it was real. But in June 2026, the Justice Department and 17 states accused three of the biggest egg producers of pushing up a benchmark price most Americans have never heard of. We follow one dozen eggs from the hen to the shelf to see who got paid. The companies denied wrongdoing and settled without admitting any. Main sources below.
