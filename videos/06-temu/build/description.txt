Temu sold two-dollar socks shipped from China for free. Cheap factories, burning cash and Super Bowl ads are only part of the story: the real trick was sending every order as its own parcel under the US "de minimis" rule, duty-free. We check each explanation against PDD's own accounts, then follow how the US closed that door in 2025 and 2026, how Temu moved to local warehouses and Europe, and whether it can survive as the same company.
