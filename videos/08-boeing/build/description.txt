In 2018, Boeing delivered more airplanes than anyone on earth. From 2019 to 2024, it lost almost $36 billion. The full timeline: the engineers' company that bet everything on the 707 and 747, the 1997 McDonnell Douglas merger, the outsourced 787 Dreamliner, the 737 MAX crashes that killed 346 people, the Alaska Airlines door plug, the 2024 strike, and Boeing's latest results. At every turning point, we put a price on the decision. Main sources below.
