In 2023, Red Lobster made all-you-can-eat shrimp a permanent $20 deal, lost $11 million on it in one quarter, and went bankrupt within a year. But its bankruptcy filing tells a longer story: buildings sold and rented back in 2014, rising rent, 30% fewer customers, and an owner that was also its shrimp supplier. Thai Union has called the filing's allegations meritless.
