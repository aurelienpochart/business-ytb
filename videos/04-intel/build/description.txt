In 2021, Intel kept almost $20 billion of profit. In 2024, it lost almost $19 billion. Today TSMC sells more than twice as much, and the US government owns about a tenth of Intel. The full timeline: the iPhone chip it passed on, the factory delays, the AI wave that went elsewhere, Pat Gelsinger's bet and the rescue. Main sources below.
